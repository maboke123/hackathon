## 1. Eligible population

- Bedienden of Veldra Belgium NV (PC 200), office Brussels and distribution centre Laakdal.
- Employees with at least 6 months of service on 1 October of the year before.
- Arbeiders at the distribution centre are not in scope. Veldra will reconsider this after the 2027 wage negotiations.
- Students, flexi-jobbers and employees in their notice period cannot take part.

About 610 employees were eligible for the 2026 round. 71% made at least one choice.

## 2. Budget

The flexible budget is built from:

| Source                                                                         | Share that can be converted |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Eindejaarspremie (13th month, PC 200)                                          | Up to 100%                  |
| Extra-legal holiday days above the 20 legal days (Veldra gives 2 company days) | Up to 2 days                |

The budget is calculated on the gross monthly salary on 1 October. Changes in salary after that date do not change the budget of the running year. The plan is cost neutral for Veldra: the employer cost of each benefit is deducted from the gross budget, including the employer RSZ saved or due.

Budget that is not spent is paid out with the December payroll as eindejaarspremie, as before the plan.

## 3. Benefit catalogue 2026

| Benefit                                     | Conditions                                                            | Payroll treatment                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Extra holiday days                          | Max 5 days per year, to be taken before 31 December                   | Salary continues, days recorded in Protime                                |
| Bike lease (including e-bike)               | 36 months, through the leasing partner, max EUR 5,000 catalogue value | Monthly deduction from the budget, bike allowance for commuting continues |
| Extra employer contribution group insurance | Within the 80% limit                                                  | Yearly contribution in December                                           |
| PC-privé plan (laptop, tablet, smartphone)  | One device per 2 years                                                | Monthly deduction, benefit in kind where applicable                       |
| Home charging station                       | Only for employees with an electric company car                       | One-off, from 2026                                                        |
| Cash                                        | Remaining budget                                                      | Paid as eindejaarspremie in December                                      |

Benefits are only offered where the tax and RSZ treatment is supported by the ruling that Veldra obtained for the plan. New options go through Legal knowledge centre Belgium before they are added.

## 4. Yearly calendar

| When             | What                                                   |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1 October        | Budget calculated, eligible employees selected         |
| 15 October       | Budget letters available in mysdworx                   |
| 1 to 30 November | Choice window in the Flex Income Plan module           |
| 10 December      | Choices locked and sent to Innovapay                   |
| January          | First monthly deductions on the payslip                |
| February         | Review with Veldra (participation, costs, new options) |

## 5. Payroll interface

- Choices flow from the Flex Income Plan module into Innovapay (Belgium) as one wage code per benefit.
- The payroll team Veldra BE checks the import report before the January run.
- Leavers: remaining budget is settled in the final pay. Benefits already delivered are not reclaimed, except bike lease (buy-out according to the leasing contract).

## 6. Points of attention

- Extra holiday days must be registered in Protime before the choice window closes, or the planning at the distribution centre does not see them.
- Veldra asks every year to add the mobility budget. Not possible in the plan: the mobility budget is a separate scheme for employees with a company car.
- Questions from employees go to Veldra HR first. SD Worx does not answer employees directly for this plan.
