# Annex B to the service agreement between Veldra Sportswear and SD Worx

Service levels, payroll calendar and escalation, Belgium and the Netherlands

|               |                                        |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Reference     | SA-VEL-2010-001, annex B, version 2025 |
| Effective     | 1 April 2025                           |
| Replaces      | Annex B version 2021                   |
| SD Worx owner | Jonas Peeters, global account owner    |

## 1. Scope

This annex applies to the payroll services that SD Worx delivers to:

- Veldra Belgium NV (distribution centre Laakdal, office Brussels), payroll on SD Worx Innovapay since 1 January 2025.
- Veldra Europe B.V. (Amsterdam), payroll on SD Worx Innovapay since 1 April 2024.

Services for other Veldra entities are covered by separate local agreements and are not part of this annex.

## 2. Monthly payroll calendar

| Step                                                        | Belgium                          | Netherlands                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Input cut-off (variable data, new hires, leavers, absences) | 18th of the month, 17:00         | 18th of the month, 17:00         |
| Pre-payroll check sent to Veldra                            | 21st                             | 21st                             |
| Approval by Veldra                                          | 23rd, 12:00                      | 22nd, 12:00                      |
| Payslips available in mysdworx                              | Second to last working day       | 25th                             |
| Payment files to the bank                                   | Two working days before pay date | Two working days before pay date |

When the 18th falls on a weekend or public holiday, the cut-off is the last working day before it. Input received after the cut-off is processed in the next monthly run, unless Veldra requests a correction run (section 4).

## 3. Service levels

| Service                                                   | Target                      | Measured            |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Payslips on time                                          | 100% of monthly runs        | Monthly             |
| Payroll accuracy (errors caused by SD Worx)               | Less than 0.5% of payslips  | Monthly             |
| Reply to payroll questions from Veldra HR                 | 1 working day               | Monthly, 95% target |
| Reply to urgent questions (payment at risk)               | 4 working hours             | Per incident        |
| Declarations (RSZ/DmfA Belgium, loonaangifte Netherlands) | On or before the legal date | Quarterly / monthly |

Service levels are reported in the quarterly service review.

## 4. Corrections and off-cycle runs

- One correction run per month per country is included.
- Additional runs are charged according to annex C (price list).
- Correction requests are sent by the Veldra payroll manager Benelux to the Veldra payroll team mailbox.

## 5. Escalation

| Level | SD Worx                                         | Contact                                                            | When                                                                               |
| ----- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | Veldra payroll team BE or NL                    | veldra.payroll.be@sdworx.example, veldra.payroll.nl@sdworx.example | All operational questions                                                          |
| 2     | Sofie Hermans, service delivery manager Benelux | sofie.hermans@sdworx.example                                       | Missed service level, payroll incident, no answer at level 1 within 2 working days |
| 3     | Jonas Peeters, global account owner             | jonas.peeters@sdworx.example                                       | Contractual or commercial issues, repeated incidents                               |

As of March 2025 the service delivery manager for Benelux is Sofie Hermans. She replaces Wim Van den Broeck as level 2 contact for Veldra.

On the Veldra side, escalations are addressed to the payroll manager Benelux and, for incidents affecting more than 20 employees, to the group HR director Europe.

## 6. Governance

- Quarterly service review with Veldra group HR and the payroll manager Benelux.
- Monthly operational call per country.
- Changes to the payroll calendar or the service levels require written agreement of both parties.
