# Veldra Sportswear account plan 2022

Account owner: Hilde Maes, Enterprise accounts
Last update: 4 November 2022 (contacts and Q4 status)

## 1. Account summary

Veldra Sportswear designs and sells sports apparel and footwear. The European group is run from Veldra Europe B.V. in Amsterdam. Veldra has been an SD Worx customer since 2010, when Veldra Belgium NV moved its payroll to us for the new distribution centre in Laakdal. For eight years this was a Belgian-only account. The Netherlands followed in 2018.

Netherlands: the Dutch team runs payroll on the legacy engine. Veldra wants one payroll platform for Benelux. The Dutch migration is planned for 2023 and will be the reference for a later Belgian move.

## 4. Service calendar and SLAs (Belgium)

- Monthly payroll input cut-off: the 20th of the month, 17:00. Changes after the cut-off go to the next month or a correction run.
- Payslips available in the portal on the second to last working day of the month.
- Correction runs: maximum one per month, on request of Mark Janssen.
- Year-end: eindejaarspremie paid with the December payroll, dubbel vakantiegeld for bedienden in May.

## 5. Escalation path

1. Payroll consultant of the Veldra team (shared mailbox, reply within one working day).
2. Wim Van den Broeck, service delivery manager Benelux.
3. Hilde Maes, global account owner.

Paul Verbeke has asked to be informed directly about any payroll incident that affects more than 20 employees.

## 6. Governance

- Quarterly service review with Paul Verbeke and Mark Janssen (Brussels or Amsterdam, alternating).
- Yearly strategic review in January with the Veldra Europe HR leadership.
- Monthly operational call Belgium between Mark Janssen and the payroll team.

## 8. Risks

- Peak season in Q4 puts pressure on the monthly cut-off, because hours and premiums for the DC come in late.
- The Dutch migration in 2023 needs Veldra HR capacity that is also needed for their own HR system project.
- Most of the knowledge about the Belgian set-up (shift premiums, cost centre mapping) sits with two people in the payroll team.

## 9. Goals for 2022

1. No late payslips and at most two correction runs for the year in Belgium.
2. Sign the Dutch migration plan by Q3.
3. Win the Spanish RFP.
4. Get a first meeting with Veldra Deutschland.
