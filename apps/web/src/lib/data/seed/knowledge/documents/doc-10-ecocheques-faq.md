# Ecocheques FAQ

Customer service Belgium, SME desk

Owner: Bram Claessens (bram.claessens@sdworx.example). Last reviewed: 20 November 2025.

## What are ecocheques?

Vouchers that employees can only spend on ecological products and services, from a fixed list set by the National Labour Council (collective agreement 98). Examples: energy efficient appliances, bicycles and their maintenance, second hand goods, organic food. Under the right conditions they are exempt from social security contributions and withholding tax.

## What are the limits?

| Rule                          | Value                              |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Maximum per employee per year | EUR 250                            |
| Maximum per voucher           | EUR 10                             |
| Validity                      | 24 months                          |
| Form                          | Electronic, on the employee's name |
| Exchange for cash             | Not allowed                        |

## Is it mandatory for our customers?

Only if a sector or company agreement says so. For white collar staff in joint committee 200 (PC 200), the employer grants EUR 250 per year for a full time employee, paid in June. The reference period runs from 1 June of the previous year to 31 May.

Employers in other joint committees: check the sector agreement. Some sectors have converted ecocheques into another benefit.

## How do we calculate it for part time staff?

Pro rata to the working time in the reference period. An employee working 4/5 for the whole period gets EUR 200. Some sectors use their own table. The engine follows the table set for the customer.

## What about someone who left during the reference period?

In PC 200 an employee who leaves gets ecocheques pro rata at the end of the contract, unless dismissed for serious cause. The amount is paid out with the final payslip run.

## Which days count?

Days worked and a list of assimilated days set by the sector agreement (for example holiday and maternity leave). Check the agreement of the customer's joint committee before answering.

## Which issuer?

Pluxee, Edenred or Monizze. The issuer is set in the customer record. The monthly file to the issuer is sent after closing.

## Can the customer give more than EUR 250?

Yes, but the part above EUR 250 is taxed as normal pay and is subject to social security.

## Can the customer replace ecocheques with something else?

Only through a company agreement that offers an equivalent benefit, and within the rules of the sector. A cafeteria plan (Flex Income Plan) can include the ecocheque budget. Refer the customer to their account manager.

## An employee says his ecocheques expired. What can we do?

Nothing. Expired vouchers are lost. Remind customers to tell their staff about the 24 month validity.
