# Checklist: onboarding as a new account owner

Enterprise accounts

Use this checklist in your first four weeks on a new key account. Tick each item and keep the list in the customer folder.

## Week 1: access and tools

- [ ] Request access to **Worxnet** and the Key Accounts space. Your manager approves the request in the Worxnet access form.
- [ ] Ask the Global Accounts desk (Antwerp, 3rd floor) for the customer file. For most accounts this is one Word document with contacts, contract summary and history.
- [ ] Request a licence for **Customer Book** (our CRM) and ask to be set as account owner on the customer record.
- [ ] Get read access to the customer folders on the Enterprise Accounts site.
- [ ] Add yourself to the account distribution list (dl-account-<customer>@sdworx.example).

## Week 1: people

- [ ] 30 minute handover call with the previous account owner. Go through the customer file together.
- [ ] Meet the payroll team lead who runs the Belgian payroll for the customer.
- [ ] For customers with entities outside Belgium: introduction by the International Payroll Coordination team (IPC), who coordinate our partner providers per country.

## Week 2: customer and contract

- [ ] Read the framework agreement and all annexes. Note the monthly cut-off dates and service levels.
- [ ] Check the invoicing set-up with Finance (billing contact, price indexation clause).
- [ ] Book an introduction meeting with the customer's HR director.
- [ ] Update the contact list in Customer Book after the meeting.

## Week 3: operations

- [ ] Walk through one full payroll cycle with the payroll team on the Belgian engine. All Belgian entities run on this engine by default. Other engines only after approval by the product board.
- [ ] Review open tickets and escalations of the last six months.
- [ ] Check which extra modules the customer uses (time registration, reporting, cafeteria plan).

## Week 4: plan

- [ ] Write or update the account plan (template on Worxnet, section Key Accounts).
- [ ] Agree the dates for the quarterly service reviews for the coming year.
- [ ] Present the account plan to your manager.

## Useful contacts

| Invoicing              | Finance, key accounts billing            |

## Tips

- Keep the customer file up to date. The next account owner will depend on it.
- Save documents in the customer folder, not on your own drive.
- Update November 2021: quarterly service reviews and handover calls now take place in Teams instead of Skype for Business. Book them from the Teams calendar.
