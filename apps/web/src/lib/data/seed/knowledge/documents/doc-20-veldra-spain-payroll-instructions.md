## 2. Monthly calendar

| Day                                                 | Step                                                                                 |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 15th                                                | Cut-off for variable data from Veldra (hours, commissions, absences, new hires)      |
| 20th                                                | Draft payroll and cost report to Veldra                                              |
| 22nd                                                | Approval by Veldra                                                                   |
| Last working day                                    | Payment of salaries (transferencia)                                                  |
| Before the last calendar day of the following month | Social security contributions through Sistema RED / SILTRA                           |
| 20th of the following month                         | Modelo 111 (IRPF withholding), monthly because Veldra Iberia files as a gran empresa |

New hires must be registered with the Seguridad Social (alta) before the first working day and communicated to the SEPE through Contrat@ within 10 days. Veldra sends the signed contract and the employee's data at least 3 working days before the start date.

## 3. Salary structure

- Salario base according to the table of the applicable convenio and professional group.
- Plus convenio and, for store staff, plus de domingos y festivos for work on Sundays and public holidays that stores open.
- Commissions for store managers, paid the month after Veldra sends the figures.
- Complemento personal for employees who came from the previous franchise partner in 2023.

Check every January that no salario base is below the SMI for the year. For 2025 the SMI tables were updated in version 1.2.

## 4. Pagas extra (extra payments)

Veldra Iberia pays 14 salaries per year: 12 monthly salaries and 2 pagas extra. All employees, office and stores, receive the pagas extra with the June and December payroll.

| Paga extra      | Paid with        | Accrual period        |
| --------------- | ---------------- | --------------------- |
| Paga de verano  | June payroll     | 1 January to 30 June  |
| Paga de Navidad | December payroll | 1 July to 31 December |

Each paga extra is one month of salario base plus the plus convenio. Employees who start or leave during the accrual period receive a prorated amount. On termination, the accrued part is paid in the finiquito.

Social security contributions are calculated monthly on the prorated pagas extra (prorrata de pagas extra in the contribution base), even though the payment itself is made in June and December.

## 5. IRPF

- Withholding percentage recalculated in January and when salary or family situation changes (modelo 145 from the employee).
- Annual summary modelo 190 in January.
- Certificates of withholding to employees by the end of February.

## 6. Absences

- Incapacidad temporal (IT): Veldra complements the benefit to 100% of salary from day 1 for office staff and from day 4 for store staff, according to the convenios.
- Birth and care of a child: benefit paid directly by the INSS, no salary during the leave. Register the leave in the payroll and send the certificate of company data (certificado de empresa) through Sistema RED.
