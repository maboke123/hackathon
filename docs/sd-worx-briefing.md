**What juries seem to value (inference, not a published criterion):**

- The two internal winners both take messy input (legal text, emails) and produce structured actions inside payroll systems. Later SD Worx shipped Legal Watch and the multi-agent payroll model, which look like the same line of work.
- Both remove manual work for payroll consultants and keep a human validation step.
- So: operational value, clear link to payroll or HR administration, human in the loop, and a path into mysdworx are likely to score well. Pure chatbots without system actions are probably less distinctive, since SD Worx already has several.
