# Spott briefing

Prepared 2026-09-29 from public sources. Source references such as [S4] point to the list at the end. Companion to `sd-worx-briefing.md`, which covers our track owner.

## 0. Read this first

- **Spott's role at the event.** Spott is the prize partner of the Tectonic Hackathon and "proudly awards EUR 10k cash to the final winner". It does not own a track: the challenges come from KBC and SD Worx. Spott is also listed as an Ecosystem Partner of the Tectonic conference (21 October 2026, Wintercircus and De Vooruit, Ghent), where co-founder and COO Manu Vanderveeren is a confirmed speaker. Whether Spott people sit on the preselection or final jury is not published. [S1] [S2]
- **Do they do HR?** Partly. Spott is not an HR, payroll or time company. It builds an AI-native ATS and CRM (applicant tracking plus client relationship management) for recruitment and staffing agencies: sourcing, matching, outreach, presenting candidates to clients and recording placements. Its product stops at the placement. Everything after that (contract, Dimona, payroll, time) is SD Worx territory. SD Worx also runs its own staffing and recruitment business (SD Worx Staffing and Career Solutions), which is the kind of firm Spott sells to. [S3] [S24] [S67]
- **Local link.** Spott is headquartered in Leuven. The three founders met as business engineering (handelsingenieur) students at KU Leuven, through the student investment club BRIKS. [S10] [S15] [S16]
- **Judging criteria are not public.** Anything in section 7 about what Spott values is inference and marked as such.
- **Reliability note.** Most company facts come from Spott's own site and its Series A release. Independent coverage (Tech.eu, TNW, Bloovi, made-in.be) repeats the same release, so it confirms what Spott said, not that it is true. Customer quotes and uplift figures are marketing. Headcount and customer counts differ between pages because they were written months apart. Dates in Spott's blog listing are unreliable (older posts show a September 2026 republish date), so dates below come from the article text. Items that could not be confirmed are marked "unverified" or "sources differ".

**What Spott seems to value (inference, not a published criterion):**

- Technical substance over labels. They mock "AI-powered" features that are "a GPT wrapper" and ask "what does your AI actually process, and when?". The CEO still codes half the week. A working flow on real-looking data will land better than slides.
- AI inside the data path, with a human deciding. Every AI suggestion in Spott shows its source and reasoning and waits for acceptance. Their best story (Flaire) is an agent that drafts, and a recruiter who validates.
- Structured data out of messy input: calls, CVs, emails into fields, reports and actions. Same pattern as SD Worx's own hackathon winners.
- Open plumbing: API, webhooks and MCP. A demo that shows how it would plug into an existing system (Spott or mysdworx) fits their thinking.
- Measurable outcomes (placements up 30%, four-week migration, hours of admin removed) and speed of shipping.
- Data ownership and privacy: EU hosting, no training on customer data.

- Use Spott's words: placement, candidate report, shortlist, match score with reasons, "Mark placed", webhook, MCP. Use SD Worx's words on the payroll side: HR, Pay and Time, mysdworx, Dimona, PC.
